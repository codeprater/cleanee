Common questions

Where do I get my clothes back?
At the hotel front desk. The app tells you when your bag is there.

How do I cancel my plan?
Open Cleanee, go to Settings, and tap Cancel plan. The Crew Plan keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for.

Can I cancel a pickup?
Yes, until we pick up your bag. On Pay Per Wash the $35 is refunded.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete account. This cancels billing and erases your profile and order history.
