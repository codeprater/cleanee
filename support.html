Common questions

Where do I get my clothes back?
At the hotel front desk. The app tells you when your bag is there.

How do I cancel my plan?
Open Cleanee, go to Settings, and tap Cancel plan. The Crew Plan keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for.

Can I cancel a pickup?
Yes, until we pick up your bag. On Pay Per Wash the $35 is refunded.

Apple Pay isn't working. What do I do?
Make sure there's a card in Apple Wallet, then try again in Account → Plan & billing. If Apple Pay still won't finish, tap "Use a card instead". Nothing is charged unless the app confirms your plan. Email us if it keeps happening.

What will the charge look like on my statement?
Cleanee charges come from our company, Prater Co. They appear as PRATER CO or PRATER CO* CLEANEE.

I'm not getting notifications.
In Cleanee, go to Account → Notifications and tap "Turn on notifications" or "Play a test notification". If alerts are off, the button there opens iOS Settings so you can allow them.

What's the bag journey map?
Open your order and tap "Bag journey map" to see where your bag was booked, picked up and left at the front desk, with times. It uses location only at those moments and only if you allow it.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete account. This cancels billing and erases your profile, order history, photos and bag journey locations.
