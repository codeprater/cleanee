Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Cleanee is a laundry service app for production staff and crew, operated by Courtney Prater ("we"). This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and a password (stored securely by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password).
  • Service details: your city, hotel, room number, laundry bag number, pickup times, service and detergent choices, and notes you add for the washer.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe, including Apple Pay. We never receive or store your full card number. We keep the Stripe customer and payment IDs needed to charge your plan and issue refunds.
  • Proof photos: when your bag is picked up or dropped at the front desk, our staff may take a photo of the bag and record the item count so you can check it. Photos are kept with your order and deleted with your account.
  • Bag journey (location): when a pickup is booked, picked up, left at the front desk or collected, the iPhone doing that step records one location reading and the nearest street address. It's saved with your order so you and our team can see your bag's journey on a map. Our staff's phones do the same for the steps in between, like washing; those locations are only visible to our team. This happens only if location access is allowed ("While Using the App"), only at those moments, and never in the background. Cleanee works fully without it.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can tell you when a pickup is booked, picked up, washing, ready and at the front desk.
  • Dry cleaner map: uses your location on your phone to show cleaners near you. That lookup isn't stored by us.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

Who processes it

Google Firebase (accounts, database, notifications) and Stripe (payments) process data for us under their own privacy terms. Apple turns a location into a street address for the bag journey. Apple Intelligence features in the app run on the iPhone and don't send what's typed to us.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. When you delete your account in Settings, we delete your profile, order history, proof photos and bag journey locations right away and cancel billing. Stripe keeps payment records it is legally required to keep.

Your choices

You can edit your details in Settings, turn notifications or location off in iOS Settings (or in Cleanee under Account → Notifications), and delete your account at any time in the app.
