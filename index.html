Laundry for the crew

Clean clothes, every city on the tour.

Cleanee is the laundry service for our touring film production. Leave your bag out at the hotel. We wash, dry and fold it and have it waiting at the front desk the same day, however late you wrap.

Get helpSee plans
How it works

Three steps. No laundromat.

1

Book a pickup

Open Cleanee, pick your service and detergent, and leave your tagged bag outside your door.

2

We wash & fold

We photograph your bag and count the items, then wash, dry and fold everything.

3

Grab it at the desk

Your bag waits at the hotel front desk. Show your 4-digit code and it's yours.

The front-desk guarantee

Know where your bag is, always.

1
Proof photos

When we pick up your bag and when we leave it at the desk.

2
Item count

Confirm it, or flag it if something looks off.

3
Handoff code

The desk only releases your bag to someone with your code.

4
Live progress

On your Lock Screen, widgets and Apple Watch.
